UInjectForge Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

UInjectForge helps you process your own game backups into customized Wii U titles. Most work happens locally on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, when it connects to other services, and what remains under your control.

Files and information stored on your device

UInjectForge stores settings, saved-base records, build history, recovery checkpoints, trial status, cached purchase entitlement, imported sources, temporary work files, and completed output on your device. Android app backup is disabled. On iOS, saved secrets and local trial and unlock state use Apple Keychain; the app also attempts to exclude its private data folder from device backups.

On desktop, data can be stored in your selected data folder, while temporary work and caches also use the operating system's temporary storage. A selected folder is not automatically private or encrypted and may be synchronized by a cloud provider. Saved keys can remain in legacy settings or use Windows account protection, macOS Keychain, or Linux Secret Service where supported. Keep data folders and their backups private; do not share settings files or credential storage.

On desktop, importing a local encrypted base creates a decrypted working copy and retains matching install-package files, including its ticket, in the local base cache for later offline packaging. Treat this cache as private input data. It is not included in public application packages or automatically attached to support bundles. Its configured folder and backups may be synchronized or stored by a provider you choose.

Remembered Assistant connections use persistent credential storage separately from ordinary desktop settings. Console-side remembered pairing is stored on the console, independently of desktop data.

Your selected game, artwork, base, runtime, and other source files are processed locally. UInjectForge does not automatically upload those files to the developer. A file provider you select may separately store or synchronize files under its own terms.

Artwork discovery and other network features

The iOS local-input edition, beginning with version 1.0.5 build 106 and including build 107, does not perform the artwork, manual, compatibility-catalog, base or support-package downloads described in the next two paragraphs. It uses files that you select and local resources. Those catalog and content-provider descriptions apply to other editions that offer those features. The iOS edition still uses Apple's App Store and iCloud services, your selected Files provider, user-initiated Wii U local-network transfers, explicit release-history refresh, and external links that you choose to open, as described in this policy.

Artwork discovery can start automatically when you select a source game, before you choose an online artwork candidate. The app can download catalog information and request matching artwork using a game identifier or a title-derived asset path. The source game file itself is not uploaded for these lookups. Downloaded catalog information and artwork can be cached locally.

Other online features can request metadata, compatibility information, manuals, or base downloads. Compatibility lookups may also run after selecting a console or game. Compatibility and manual catalog matching happens locally after downloading catalog data. UIF community compatibility refresh downloads complete reviewed-report publications from GitHub Pages and the UIF community report service hosted on Cloudflare Workers. These requests do not include the selected game’s name, identifier, or local path. Current providers include GitHub-hosted community repositories, Libretro, GameTDB, Nintendo content-delivery infrastructure, and custom endpoints you configure. These services receive connection information, such as your IP address, and the requested URL or asset path. Artwork requests can include filename-derived game titles. Some requests identify the app as UInjectForge. Providers may retain request information under their own policies; UInjectForge does not control their logging or retention. Some legacy Wii U content-delivery requests use unencrypted HTTP and should not be treated as confidential.

In iOS and Android 1.1.1, choosing Refresh history in What's New requests public release-note text from a fixed, platform-specific address on the project's GitHub Pages Community site. This request does not include your game files or identifiers, local paths, keys, build settings, purchase information, or installed-version value. The host receives ordinary connection information, including the requested platform address and HTTP headers, and may retain it under its own policies. UIF saves validated notes in its app data for offline display and retains bundled notes when a refresh is unavailable. Mobile startup does not automatically request this history. This metadata refresh does not download or install software or game content.

If you enter and confirm a hosted patch-collection URL, UIF requests the JSON file from that HTTPS publisher. The publisher receives the URL and normal connection information. Matching collection entries against your game executable happens locally; the collection downloader does not upload your game or executable.

Wii U discovery, HTTP transfer, FTP transfer, and Assistant features communicate with the console or endpoint you select. Transfers send the files you select to that console. Requests can include pairing credentials and device/title status. Ordinary HTTP and FTP do not encrypt traffic; an Assistant access token authorizes commands but does not encrypt them. FTP encryption depends on your selected settings and server support. Use these features only on a network you trust. Opening an external website also subjects that visit to the site's own privacy practices.

Google Play purchases

The Android app offers three successful builds and a one-time Google Play purchase for unlimited builds. Google Play processes the purchase and provides entitlement information to the app. UInjectForge stores the successful-build count and last confirmed entitlement in protected app-private storage. If you select a shared output folder, UIF also stores a small JSON receipt there containing the count, update time, app ID, and a one-way hash of Android's app-signing/user/device-scoped identifier. UIF does not send this receipt to the developer. The document provider you choose controls the folder and may sync the receipt if it is cloud-backed. Reselecting the folder can restore known usage after clearing app data or reinstalling; deleting or never creating the receipt can still reset the local count. The developer does not operate a UInjectForge account or licensing server.

When the Android app opens or returns to the foreground, UInjectForge asks Google Play to check ownership of Unlimited Builds and retrieve its localized price. Google Play handles checkout and payment details. The Google Play Billing Library can send operational diagnostics to Google, including the app version, device model, Android version, and network connection information. UInjectForge uses purchase tokens to acknowledge purchases through Google Play. Google processes billing data under its own privacy policy.

Apple App Store purchases and iCloud trial status

The iOS app offers three successful builds and a separate one-time App Store purchase for unlimited builds. Apple processes the purchase. UInjectForge uses StoreKit to obtain product information, verify the unlock entitlement, and respond to purchase or entitlement changes. Payment-card details are not provided to UInjectForge. The app keeps local trial and confirmed unlock state in Apple Keychain. The developer does not operate a UInjectForge account, purchase-verification server, or trial-tracking backend.

When available, Apple's iCloud key-value store synchronizes three Boolean values indicating whether free build slots 1, 2, and 3 have been consumed. The values themselves contain no game files, keys, purchase tokens, device identifiers, or account identifiers. Apple associates the iCloud service with your Apple Account; these values are not sent to a developer-operated server. Synchronization depends on Apple's services and your iCloud availability. Apple operates iCloud and the App Store under its own privacy terms.

Support information

Support bundles and diagnostic reports are created locally, including reports generated by enabled debug diagnostics. Exporting a bundle saves it to the location you choose; it does not automatically send the bundle to the developer. A bundle can include the app version and build, game or base names, build activity, error details, environment information, and text diagnostics. UInjectForge attempts to redact keys and private paths and excludes source game files and other binary payloads from the bundle. Review the exported information before sharing it.

The developer receives information you voluntarily send for support, such as your email address, message, and any diagnostic attachments. That information is used to respond to your request and investigate the reported problem. Information you voluntarily send for support is retained only as needed to investigate the report and maintain project records. Contact the email address below about support information you have sent, including a request to delete it.

Advertising, analytics, and accounts

UInjectForge does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics and does not create a UInjectForge account. The developer does not automatically receive your files, build history, trial count, or diagnostic bundles. Apple services, other platform services, and external providers have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

UInjectForge's storage controls can remove supported temporary files, caches and selected build data. Closing or deleting the desktop executable does not remove your configured data/output folders, operating-system credential entries or console-side remembered pairing. Use the app's Forget option and Assistant's device-management controls to remove remembered connections; deleting desktop files alone does not revoke console-side trust. Keep or remove other desktop data and backups deliberately.

On mobile, removing local files does not reset trial usage or erase an App Store purchase. Uninstalling the app removes app-private files according to the operating system, but Keychain state, App Store purchase records, and the three iCloud trial flags may remain available. The app does not provide a control to reset the iCloud trial flags. Files that you export to another folder, cloud provider, SD card, or Wii U remain there until you delete them.

Children

UInjectForge is a technical utility intended for people who can lawfully manage their own game backups. It is not designed for children.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when app features or service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
