A small first step

Make it your wheel.

  1. Give it a spin

    Choose Try the wheel, then Spin the wheel. Six starter activities are ready. No setup or permissions needed.

  2. Add what you love

    Open Activities to edit a starter or add your own. Already know what to do? Choose Do this now from its menu.

  3. Pause apps, if you like

    App pause can pause the apps you choose with Screen Time. It’s optional, and you can turn it off anytime.

A little help

Good to know.

Replay the short guide from Settings → Getting started whenever you need it.

How do I set up app pause?

Open App pause → Connect Screen Time and allow access. Choose your apps, save, then turn on Pause selected apps. App pause needs a real iPhone or iPad.

You can use the wheel without connecting Screen Time. For connection issues, open Settings → App pause help.

How do I take an app break?

Spin, then explicitly choose the offered app break. Spinning alone never unlocks apps. With Try it first on, finish the short starting timer before choosing a break.

Timers and notifications never unlock apps automatically. Starting another activity during a break pauses the selected apps again.

An app still opens, or a break hasn’t ended

In App pause, check that the app is selected and Pause selected apps is on. Selected apps can open during an active break.

Open Wanna Be to recheck an expired break, or choose Pause apps again now. iOS may apply the pause when you next use your device.

How do I stop pausing apps?

Open Wanna Be from your Home Screen or App Library and switch off Pause selected apps in App pause. You can also revoke its Screen Time access in iOS Settings.

The shield’s Close this app button closes the paused app; it does not open Wanna Be.

Why does my wheel have no choices?

Open the set dropdown on Wheel to check Set & filters. Make sure some activities fit your time, energy, and place and are included on the wheel.

Skip today and Unavailable today hide an activity until tomorrow. Its menu in Activities can make it available again today.

Can I save or move my wheel?

Use Settings → Wheel backup to export activities and sets. Import shows a preview and keeps your current activities. History, timers, preferences, and app-pause settings are not transferred.

Undo in Activities restores the most recently deleted activity, including after reopening the app.

Can I change the appearance or text size?

Choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings. Wanna Be follows larger system text and Reduce Motion, and has an optional Haptics switch.

Where can I get the app?

Wanna Be is currently in testing. There is no public App Store release yet. If you’ve received a TestFlight invitation, follow the instructions in that invitation.
