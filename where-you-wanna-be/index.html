A small first step

Make it your wheel.

  1. Give it a spin

    Choose Try the wheel, then Spin the wheel. Six starter activities are ready. Unlimited spins are free, with no purchase or enrollment needed.

  2. Add what you love

    Open Activities to edit, add, or search your hobbies. Already know what to do? Choose Do this now from its menu. Settings shows what you marked complete this week.

  3. Pause apps, if you like

    App pause can pause one individual app for free using Screen Time. Lifetime Upgrade adds multiple apps, categories, and web domains. You can turn it off anytime.

A little help

Good to know.

Replay the short guide from Settings → Getting started whenever you need it.

How do I plan my day?

Open Today to add a task for any day, with an optional time, reminder, checklist, or smaller first step. Routines repeat familiar tasks. Choose a task to start its steps or timer; a timer never marks it done for you.

You can show existing calendar events or review a separate copy with Add to Calendar. Calendar and Today do not sync changes. Choose your opening tab in Settings.

Can someone send me a task?

Choose Share task from a task’s menu, or create or review a task file or link in your browser. A bigger task can include its groups and subtasks together. The recipient reviews the title, steps, and suggested time before adding it to Today. Reminders are their choice; sharing never assigns a task automatically or starts a timer.

Task-file opening requires an app build with task-file sharing; older task-sharing builds can still use web links. On Android or other devices, the browser works without an app or shared inbox: review a task file or link, copy the details, or choose a calendar copy. Anyone with the file or link can read the task details.

How do I set up app pause?

Open App pause → Connect Screen Time and allow access. Choose an individual app, save, then turn on Pause selected apps. One app is free; multiple apps, categories, and web domains need Lifetime Upgrade. Screen Time needs a real iPhone or iPad.

You can use the wheel without connecting Screen Time. For connection issues, open Settings → App pause help.

How do I take an app break?

Spin, then explicitly choose the offered app break. Spinning alone never unlocks apps. With Try it first on, finish the short starting timer before choosing a break.

Timers and notifications never unlock apps automatically. Starting another activity during a break pauses the selected apps again.

An app still opens, or a break hasn’t ended

In App pause, check that the app is selected and Pause selected apps is on. Selected apps can open during an active break.

Open Wanna Be to recheck an expired break, or choose Pause apps again now. iOS may apply the pause when you next use your device.

How do I stop pausing apps?

Switch off Pause selected apps in App pause, or use Turn off app pause in Settings. You can also revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings. If upgrade access is lost, saved selections stay manageable and removable; new selections must fit the free one-app limit.

The shield’s Close this app button closes the paused app; it does not open Wanna Be.

Why does my wheel have no choices?

Open the set dropdown on Wheel to check Set & filters. Make sure some activities fit your time, energy, and place and are included on the wheel.

Skip today and Unavailable today hide an activity until tomorrow. Its menu in Activities can make it available again today.

Can I save or move my wheel?

Use Settings → Wheel backup to export activities, sets, and filters saved with sets. New exports need Wanna Be 0.9.1 or later to import; older backups still work. Import previews additions and keeps your current activities. History, timers, ordinary wheel filters, appearance, and app-pause settings are not transferred.

Undo restores the last deleted activity. Hobbies, timers, history, and backups are free. Saved sets require Lifetime Upgrade. They can optionally remember time, energy, and place; existing sets keep current filters until you opt in. Duplicate opens an editable copy before Save. The Saved wheel widget opens a chosen set; it never spins or unlocks apps, and an existing timer takes priority. If upgrade access is lost, sets stay saved but inactive.

Can I change the appearance or text size?

Choose System, Light, or Dark free in Settings. Original stays free; Lifetime Upgrade adds Coastal, Dusk, Rose, Ember, and Slate. Preview all six in light and dark in the compact theme gallery. Settings → App icons has separate previews and an explicit Apply action. Original is free; the upgrade adds five matching icons. Wanna Be follows larger text and Reduce Motion, with an optional Haptics switch. The larger iPad wheel and activity grids adapt to your window.

Where can I get the app, and what will it cost?

Wanna Be is in testing, with no public App Store release yet. The wheel, activity editing, timers, Today plans, routines, task sharing, standard appearance, and one individual app pause are free. The one-time Lifetime Upgrade, planned at US$4.99, adds saved sets and their widgets, multiple apps/categories/web domains, and five extra themes with matching app icons. Search, weekly recap, Original, and backups stay free. Apple shows your local price; restore the upgrade with the same Apple Account. No purchase trial, subscription, app account, ads, or tracking.
