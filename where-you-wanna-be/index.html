Start with what you need

Help me choose

Open Wheel and spin. Starter activities are ready; edit them in Activities to make them yours.

Help me plan

Open Today and add one task. A time, reminder, steps, and a smaller start are all optional. You can build up a full day when you want to.

Choose your opening screen in Settings. Replay the guide from Settings → Getting started.

Tasks that fit your day

Use a bigger task to collect independent subtasks, then schedule and check off each part. Paste a list to break it down, or add more subtasks later. Routines repeat familiar tasks; copying a day gives another day the same starting plan.

Sharing starts in the app. Open Today and choose Send a task. You can also share a task you already made. A bigger task can include its subtasks in one file. The recipient reviews a separate copy and chooses whether and when to add it.

Help and common questions

How do I send or open a task file?

From a task in the app, choose Share and send the named .wannabetask attachment in a message or email. You can include its subtasks and optional suggested timing.

Open the attachment with Wanna Be. If your messaging or file app does not offer it, save the file and choose Today → Import task in Wanna Be. Review before adding it; reminders and completion never transfer automatically.

The browser task tools are a fallback for people without the app. Web links are still available, but task files keep messages easier to read. Later edits and progress do not sync between people.

How do Today and my calendar work together?

Today works without calendar permission. You can optionally show existing events and review a time block with Add to Calendar. Calendar and Today keep separate copies; edits or completion in one do not change the other.

Starting a task opens its steps or timer. A timer never marks it done for you. A reminder never starts a task or pauses or unlocks apps.

How does app pause work?

On iPhone and iPad, App pause uses Apple’s Screen Time permission to pause selected apps. One individual app is free; expanded selections require Lifetime Upgrade. It needs a real device.

Spinning alone never opens a paused app. After choosing an activity, explicitly choose the offered app break. With Try it first on, finish the short starting timer first. You can turn off app pause at any time.

On Android, app pause uses an optional accessibility service to notice a selected app and offer a local focus screen. It can help interrupt opening an app, but it is less strict than Apple’s Screen Time shield. You can turn the service off in device Settings.

An app still opens, or a break hasn’t ended

Check that the app is selected and app pause is on. Selected apps can open during an active break. Open Wanna Be to recheck an expired break, or choose Pause apps again now.

For iPhone and iPad connection issues, use Settings → App pause help. Turn off app pause in Wanna Be or revoke Screen Time access in device Settings to stop pausing.

Why does my wheel have no choices?

Open the set dropdown on Wheel and check Set & filters. Make sure some activities fit your time, energy, and place and are included on the wheel.

Skip today and Unavailable today hide an activity until tomorrow. Its menu in Activities can make it available again today.

Can I save or move my wheel?

Use Settings → Wheel backup to export and import activities and saved sets. Import previews additions and keeps your existing activities. Wheel backups work between compatible iOS and Android builds.

Wheel backups do not transfer Today plans, routines, history, timers, appearance, or device permissions. Use task sharing to send a particular task or task group.

Can I change colors, text size, or the layout?

Choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings. Theme choices include Original, Coastal, Dusk, Rose, Ember, Slate, and Amethyst. App icons have their own selection.

The app supports larger text and Reduce Motion. Layouts adapt to smaller phones, tablets, and wider windows. If a label or control is difficult to use, send a screenshot and your text-size setting with feedback.

Where can I get the app, and what costs money?

There is no public store release yet. Invited iPhone and iPad testers install through TestFlight; Android testers use the private APK shared by the developer.

The wheel, activity editing, timers, plans, routines, task sharing, standard appearance, and backups are free. On iOS, the planned US$4.99 one-time Lifetime Upgrade adds saved sets, expanded app-pause selections, six extra themes, and five extra app icons. Apple shows your local price; restore with the same Apple Account.

Android purchases are not available in the private test APK. There is no subscription, app account, advertising, or tracking.

Support and feedback

Tell us what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened. Include your app version, device model, and iOS or Android version. Settings → Share feedback prepares version details; you choose how to send it.

TestFlight testers can also choose Send Beta Feedback. Leave out personal task or calendar details unless they are needed to explain the problem.
