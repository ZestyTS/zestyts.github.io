Saved on your device

Where You Wanna Be, shown as Wanna Be on your Home Screen, is made by Zesty Fernandez (ZestyTS). The app has no accounts, ads, analytics SDKs, or developer-operated server.

It saves your activities, prompts, sets, preferences, recent activity history, and current timer locally so they work between launches. The developer does not receive this information automatically. Your device’s backups may include it.

Optional app pause and reminders

With your permission, Apple’s Screen Time picker lets you select apps, categories, and websites to pause. Wanna Be saves Apple-provided selection tokens and break settings on your device, shared with its own Screen Time extensions. It does not read your messages, social feeds, or browsing history, or send your selections to the developer.

You can turn off App pause in Wanna Be and revoke its Screen Time access in iOS Settings.

If enabled, timer reminders are local notifications. Their text does not include your activity title or prompt. Turn them off in Wanna Be Settings or manage notification permission in iOS Settings. The Home Screen widget contains no saved activity information.

Sharing is your choice

Wheel exports contain activities and sets, including your written prompts. They exclude history, timers, app-pause selections, and preferences.

Share feedback prepares your note, app version/build, and iOS version. You choose the recipient and service. Nothing is sent automatically. Files and messages you share are then handled by the recipient and service you choose.

Keeping and deleting information

You can edit or delete activities and clear activity history in Settings. Deleting an activity retains the most recent undo copy and does not erase its past history. The app may preserve recovery copies when saved data cannot be read.

To remove the app and its local data, turn off App pause and delete Wanna Be rather than offloading it. Delete exported files and device backups separately if you no longer want those copies.

Support and beta testing

If you email the developer, your message and contact details are used to respond and improve the app. Support correspondence may remain in the developer’s email service. You can request deletion by contacting ZestyTS@gmail.com.

During TestFlight testing, Apple also provides testing information, crash reports, and submitted feedback to the developer. That information is used to improve the app and is handled through Apple’s service. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy information.

This support website

These pages use GitHub Pages hosting. We do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking scripts. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security when serving Pages sites. Read GitHub’s Pages hosting information and privacy statement for its practices.

Following an external link or sending an email uses the service you choose and its privacy practices.

Questions or requests

Contact Zesty Fernandez at ZestyTS@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests about information you have sent.

Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page, with an updated date.
